# **Intro to Soldering**

## **Wiring**

Soldering two wires together is going to be a fundamental aspect of this project. A proper connection needs to be established in order for data or power or whatever to be properly transferred between items. 

## **Ports**

This will be the other major soldering component of this project. Ensuring a solid connection between a wire and a port is essential, as it proper orientation of a port. In the following I will go through the proper soldering of wires to the type of port we are going to practice on before you are allowed free rein to work on your own ports.

## **Breadboards and other Components**

Unless you all decide to go a very interesting and unexpected direction with this project this week, we will likely not be working with any breadboards or other board type components that need anything directly soldered onto them. However, the basic principles of soldering both wires and ports apply. Generally, just be careful while trying to keep your solders smooth and cleaning up any excess materials.
